🛡️ Block Merge Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · V8 preparation

Release checklist: Before Google Play publication, replace the developer/contact placeholders below with the exact developer identity shown in the Play Store listing and host this policy on a public HTTPS webpage.

1. Game progress

The current web preview stores game progress, settings, scores, currencies, cosmetics and related gameplay data locally on the device. The preview does not upload this data to a server.

2. Google Play Games and cloud save

The V8 web preview contains only an integration interface for future Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save. No Google account is created or connected in the web preview. If cloud save is enabled in a future Android release, the policy will describe the data synchronized and how it is handled.

3. Advertising

The V8 web preview does not contain an active advertising SDK. A future Android build may use rewarded/interstitial advertising. Any data processed by the advertising SDK and any consent choices required for personalized advertising will be disclosed and handled through the applicable consent flow.

4. Purchases

The V8 web preview does not process real-money purchases. Future Nova Gem purchases are planned through Google Play Billing. Purchase identifiers/tokens needed to validate transactions may be processed for purchase verification. Full payment card details are not handled by Block Merge Rush itself.

5. Data sharing

The web preview does not intentionally transmit player data to third parties. Future Android integrations such as Google Play Games, Google Play Billing and advertising services may process data according to their service requirements; this policy and the Play Console Data safety form must be updated to match the exact production SDK configuration.

6. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until the player resets app data, removes the app/data, or uses an in-game reset function. If account-based cloud storage is enabled later, a clear deletion method must be provided for applicable account-associated data.

7. Children and sensitive data

The game should not request unnecessary sensitive device permissions. Any future age-related or child-directed configuration must be reviewed before release, particularly when ads are enabled.
